MPhys projects

Ising superconductivity in monolayer MoS2

I built a tight-binding model with spin-orbit coupling and a Zeeman term, then solved the linearised gap equation to calculate how the in-plane upper critical field varies with temperature.

Upper critical fields in AsxP1−x thin layers

I used a multiband slab model and a finite-thickness Ginzburg-Landau description to study how temperature, field angle and layer thickness affect the upper critical field.
