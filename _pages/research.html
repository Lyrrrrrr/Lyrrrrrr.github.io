Research

I study how superconductivity and magnetism interact in quantum materials. At the Tsung-Dao Lee Institute, my work focuses on what local magnetic probes and thermodynamic measurements can tell us about superconducting states.

Muon spectroscopy and local magnetism

I analyse muon spin rotation and relaxation (μSR) data, comparing relaxation models and the temperature and field dependence of their fitted parameters. I am particularly interested in distinguishing signatures of magnetic ordering from spontaneous fields that emerge below the superconducting transition.

Heat capacity and superconducting gaps

I fit the temperature dependence of the electronic heat capacity using two-band models to investigate the gap energy scales and their contributions to the measured response.

Modelling superconductivity in thin layers

I use tight-binding and Ginzburg-Landau models to study the upper critical field. I focus on how spin-orbit coupling, layer thickness and field orientation affect the field at which superconductivity is suppressed.
